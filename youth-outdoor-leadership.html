Three Pillars of Outdoor Leadership

Why Outdoors? Why Korea?

Korea's mountain trails are living classrooms. Every steep ridge, river crossing, and foggy summit demands real decisions with real consequences — the kind that build genuine leadership, not just confidence in theory.

Working together to navigate unfamiliar terrain, participants discover how to communicate under pressure, support each other, and lead with empathy — skills that transfer directly back to school, work, and life.

Leaving behind devices and distractions, participants reconnect with a slower, more deliberate pace. This analogue experience builds clarity, resilience, and a deeper sense of self.

🧭 Decision-Making Under Pressure

Real terrain demands real choices. Participants practice risk assessment and judgment in a safe, guided environment.

🤝 Team Dynamics & Communication

From route planning to load-sharing, every task requires collaboration, listening, and trust.

💪 Self-Reliance & Resilience

Push through fatigue, weather, and uncertainty — and discover what you're actually capable of.

🌱 Leave No Trace Ethics

Responsible outdoor leadership means protecting the natural spaces we learn in for future generations.

Program Options

Choose the format that fits your group's goals and schedule.

1–2 Days

Day & Overnight Challenge

An intensive introduction to outdoor leadership. Perfect for school groups, corporate teams, or first-time participants.

  • Trail Navigation: Map reading, route planning, and decision-making on the move
  • Team Challenges: Structured activities designed to surface leadership dynamics
  • Debrief Sessions: Guided reflection connecting outdoor experience to real-life leadership
  • Duration: 1 full day or 1 night / 2 days
3–4 Days

Leadership Expedition

Our flagship multi-day residential program. Participants rotate leadership roles, manage group decisions, and develop genuine outdoor competence. Ideal for DofE Expeditions.

  • Expedition Planning: Participants co-design the itinerary and carry full responsibility
  • Overnight Camping: Lightweight backpacking with off-grid bivouac nights
  • Leadership Journal: Daily reflection to consolidate growth and insights
  • Duration: 3–4 days (customizable)

Open Enrollment

All programs are available for groups of 4 or more participants. Custom dates available — contact us to plan your program.

What Participants Learn

A structured outdoor curriculum designed to build real-world leadership skills.

① Planning & Navigation

  • Map & Compass: Terrain reading, route selection, and bearing
  • Risk Assessment: Identifying hazards and making safe group decisions
  • Contingency Planning: Preparing for weather changes and unexpected situations

② Team Leadership

  • Role Rotation: Every participant leads a section of the expedition
  • Communication: Clear, calm, and effective communication under physical stress
  • Conflict Resolution: Managing group tension and reaching shared decisions

③ Outdoor Living Skills

  • Camp Setup: Shelter selection, tent pitching, and site management
  • Trail Cooking: Lightweight meal preparation and nutrition on the move
  • LNT Practice: Leave No Trace ethics applied throughout the expedition
