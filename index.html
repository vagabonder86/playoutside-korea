Who We Are

Education-First.
Always Outdoors.

PlayOutside Korea is an outdoor education cooperative dedicated to introducing Korea's diverse nature and rich culture through authentic wilderness experiences. We believe that true learning and deep cultural connection happen when we step beyond the classroom doors and into the wild.

From the tranquil currents of the Hongcheon River to the rugged ridges of long-distance trails, we utilize Korea's pristine environments as a living classroom. By combining a structured curriculum of hiking, backpacking, canoeing, and camping with Smart Wilderness Navigation (orienteering & geocaching) and strict Leave No Trace (LNT) ethics, we empower youth and international communities to build lifelong resilience, teamwork, and self-reliance while discovering the true essence of Korea.

📚

Education

Every outing has a learning framework — ecological literacy, risk management, self-reliance — not just a destination.

🧭

Humanistic Approach

We connect participants with Korea's cultural and natural heritage — the 산 (mountain spirit), seasonal rhythms, and traditional ecological knowledge.

🌱

Sustainable Outdoor Education

Leave No Trace principles, local ecosystem stewardship, and low-impact practices are woven into everything we do.
