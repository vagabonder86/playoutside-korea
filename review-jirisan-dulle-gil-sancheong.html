Jirisan Dulle-gil, Sancheong: Two Days at the Foot of Jirisan

Golden rice terraces, a stamp booklet to fill, and a night in a forest campsite. What the Dulle-gil is like for a first overnight hike.

By PlayOutside Korea · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Trip: Oct 3–4, 2026 · 2 min read

The short version

The Jirisan Dulle-gil is not a summit hike. It is a walk around the foot of Jirisan, through villages, fields and forest, which makes it one of the friendliest ways to try an overnight hike in Korea. In early October the rice terraces of Sancheong turn gold, and collecting trail stamps along the way gives the day a playful rhythm.

At a glance

Where
Sancheong, Gyeongnam
Trail
Jirisan Dulle-gil
When we went
Oct 3–4, 2026
Trip length
1 night, 2 days, carrying everything in one backpack
Overnight
Tents at a forest campsite

On the trail

Jirisan is one of Korea's best-known mountains, but the Dulle-gil keeps you low, on paths that link the villages around it. We walked with full packs, and everyone found their own pace first, then settled into the pace of the group.

The highlight in early autumn is the farmland. The trail runs past terraced rice fields, and in the first week of October they were just turning gold. Because the route passes through working farms and villages, it feels less like a wilderness hike and more like a slow walk through rural Korea.

The stamp booklet

Along the Dulle-gil there are stamp points, and filling a stamp booklet turns the walk into a small treasure hunt. Reading the map to find the next stamp point is a good, low-pressure way to practise navigation, and a nice souvenir to take home.

A night in the forest

We spent the night at a forest campsite. Everyone pitched their own tent and learned the knots to hold it, and dinner was a shared table full of stories from the trail. In the morning we packed up and left the site the way we found it.

What we liked

  • Golden rice terraces in early October
  • Stamp points that make navigation fun
  • A good first overnight hike, low on the mountain rather than over the summit
  • Village paths that show a side of Korea most visitors never see

Keep in mind

  • The trail passes through farms and villages: stay on the path and keep noise down
  • For an overnight trip, everything for two days goes in one pack, so pack light
  • Camp only at a campsite, and take all your rubbish home
