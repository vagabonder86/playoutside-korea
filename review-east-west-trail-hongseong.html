Trail Review

East–West Trail Section 10 and Baekwolsan, Hongseong

A climb toward Baekwolsan, a trail shelter for practising camp skills, and wide views over the autumn fields of Hongseong.

By PlayOutside Korea · Published Nov 26, 2026 · Trip: Nov 4–5, 2026 · 2 min read

The short version

This stretch of the East–West Trail packs a lot into two days: a climb toward Baekwolsan, a trail shelter where you can practise camp skills, and a ridge with wide views over the autumn fields of Hongseong. It is a good next step for hikers who have done a night or two outdoors and want to do it better.

Up toward Baekwolsan

The East–West Trail is a long-distance route across Korea, and Section 10 takes you up toward Baekwolsan in Hongseong. On the way up we practised using trekking poles: used well, they take weight off your knees and help you keep a steady rhythm with a full pack.

The trail shelter

On Section 10 there is a trail shelter near Baekwolsan, and it makes a good base for practising the skills that matter on an overnight trip: choosing a site, pitching a tent, packing, and using a stove safely.

We camped among the pines, with everyone pitching a tent with their own hands. November nights in the mountains are cold, so a warm sleeping bag, a good mat and proper layers make the difference between a long night and a good one.

The view from the ridge

The reward is the ridge. From the Baekwolsan ridge, the autumn fields of Hongseong open up below you.

What we liked

  • Wide views over Hongseong from the ridge
  • A trail shelter that makes skills practice easy
  • Camping among the pines

Keep in mind

  • Bring trekking poles for the climb
  • November nights are cold: pack layers and a warm sleeping bag
  • Use stoves only where it is safe and allowed, and never leave them unattended
