What is Backpacking School?

Everything you need for two days goes into one backpack. We walk together, camp in the forest, and practice the skills that make backpacking safe, light and respectful of nature.

🎒 Pack light

Choose what to carry, and what to leave behind.

⛺ Pitch your own

Every participant sets up their own tent and ties their own knots.

🥾 Walk together

Find your own pace, then the pace of the group.

🌱 Leave No Trace

Leave every site the way we found it.

Recap 01

Backpacking School in Jirisan

📅 Oct 3–4, 2026📍 Sancheong, Gyeongnam🥾 Jirisan Dulle-gil

Walk, eat, sleep, connect. Two days at the foot of Jirisan with everything we needed in one backpack.

Day 1 · Gather

We started in a circle

On the first morning we sat in a circle on the grass between the tents. We shared names, agreed on the promises we would keep for two days, and went through the safety rules together.

Day 1 · Pack

What to bring, and what to leave

In front of the tents and gear we covered the basics: what to pack, what to leave out, and how to load a backpack. Two days in one backpack starts with carrying less.

Day 1 · Walk

Onto the Jirisan Dulle-gil

With packs on, we headed out onto the Jirisan Dulle-gil. In early October the rice terraces of Sancheong were turning gold, and step by step everyone found their own pace, then the pace of the group.

Day 1 · Explore

One stamp at a time

Along the way we collected Dulle-gil trail stamps. Reading the map to find the next stamp point gave the walk its own rhythm.

Day 1 · Camp

A night in the forest

At the forest campsite everyone pitched their own tent and learned the knots to hold it. Dinner was a shared table, with stories about the trail, the hard moments and the views.

Day 2 · Leave No Trace

Waking up in a tent

We woke to the morning air of the forest, packed up our tents and gear, and returned the site to how we found it. Leave No Trace is as basic to backpacking as the backpack itself.

Recap 02

Backpacking School: Field Edition

📅 Nov 4–5, 2026📍 Hongseong, Chungnam🥾 East–West Trail, Section 10

For people already backpacking who want to know they are doing it right: a hands-on night on the trail in Hongseong.

Day 1 · Gather

Shouldering the pack

We began with an orientation, then put our own backpacks on the scale. Knowing what you carry is the first step to carrying it well.

Day 1 · Gear

First, weigh your pack

Choosing gear, splitting the load and layering for autumn in the mountains. The basics, done right, for people who were already backpacking but wanted to be sure.

Day 1 · Trail

Up toward Baekwolsan

We set out toward Baekwolsan and practiced using trekking poles on the way up.

Day 1 · Shelter

Learning at the shelter

At the trail shelter on Section 10 of the East–West Trail, we covered core backpacking skills: picking a site, pitching tents, packing, and using stoves safely.

Day 1 · Camp

Your tent, your hands

Among the pines, everyone set up a tent with their own hands.

Ridge

On the ridge

From the Baekwolsan ridge, the autumn fields of Hongseong opened up below us.
